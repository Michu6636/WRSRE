ADD THESE LINES TO  tesmioloader\build\plugins\resources.ini
============================================================

This is the ONE file you cannot just copy over, because other mods put their
resources in it too. Open your existing resources.ini and paste the block below
into it. If you do not have one, resources.dll makes it on first run - launch
once, then come back.

Small arms, heavy weapons and munitions are not base-game resources.


==================== PASTE ALL OF THIS. ALL OF IT. ====================

The three [list] lines go under your existing [list] section. The three
[custom:] blocks can sit anywhere below it - but they are NOT optional, and
leaving them out is the one mistake that crashes the game rather than just
doing nothing. Paste the whole block and you cannot get it wrong.

small_arms         = eletronics, Small Arms
large_arms         = eletronics, Heavy Weapons
munitions          = eletronics, Munitions

[custom:small_arms]
cargo = none

[custom:large_arms]
cargo = none

[custom:munitions]
cargo = none

======================= END OF THE PASTE BLOCK ========================


IF YOU SKIP THE [custom:] BLOCKS, THE GAME CRASHES
--------------------------------------------------

Not "produces nothing" - crashes, the moment you place a building that stores
one of these resources. The small munitions plant is usually the first one
people hit.

Why: the three are cloned from `eletronics`, which has no cargo geometry.
Without `cargo = none` the setting defaults to `auto`, the loader loads a mesh
that is not there and writes it through a null pointer. The crash happens
inside C3DDLL64.dll where nothing points back at this mod. The only clue is one
line in tesmioloader.log:

    resource "small_arms": cargo mesh load faulted

If your game crashes when placing an arms factory, check resources.ini for the
three [custom:] blocks before anything else. That is the cause nine times in
ten.


IF THE LINES ARE MISSING ENTIRELY
---------------------------------

Different symptom, no crash: the arms factories build, staff up, and produce
nothing at all, with no error anywhere.


THE ICONS
---------

They travel as a crate, like eletronics does. The PNG icons are the only art
they need, and those are already in place if you copied the tesmioloader folder.
