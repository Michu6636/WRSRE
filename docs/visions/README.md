# Visions Index

Product-level architecture for the mod. For the ordered plan of what to build
next, see [`../ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md).

- [economy-direction-2026-11-22.md](economy-direction-2026-11-22.md) — **owner decisions of 2026-11-22; overrides the other visions where they conflict** (native prices only, single domestic market, no state dispatch procurement, company export revenue, live B2B billing, no sample data, decrees, housing by wealth, two-tier pay, balancing invariants, Ghidra queue)
- [vision-private-export-markets.md](vision-private-export-markets.md) — **later step (D41), own branch:** Almanac foreign-trade page (routes per customs house, bans, duties, tariffs, quotas), measured company export revenue and import cost, price-derived exchange rate with currency surrender, export freight paid by the foreign market, company health ladder dependency; investigation of how customs trade works in the game, real life and other games
- [vision-private-logistics.md](vision-private-logistics.md) — freight, fuel, producer settlement, bounded opt-in DO targets
- [vision-private-construction.md](vision-private-construction.md) — **tentative, not scheduled (D13):** who pays (client) and who builds are separate, and ownership follows the client. The state can build private sites (O1). Workdays are billed to the client and paid to the workers (O2), with materials and workdays paid as the work is done (O3). No instant build for private payers; unpaid sites stop; 8 % contractor fee; stop after the money runs out (B); construction is its own wage sector (default 3.75 RUB/day). Privatised construction offices use the private logistics system. Also records the private-DO-to-construction-site bug
- [vision-whole-mod.md](vision-whole-mod.md) — 15-plugin economy, dependency direction, roadmap
