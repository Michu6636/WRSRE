# Native row click-close investigation: independent review

The [Windows agent's initial report](../export/almanac-evidence/native-click-close-agent-6e73439/report.md) from `6e73439` is preserved byte-for-byte. [Independent excerpts](../export/almanac-evidence/native-click-close-independent/click-paths.asm) come from existing tracked disassemblies. This is static analysis: it does not prove any path ran, and no game or plugin was executed or modified here.

## Supported planning concern

A change in `game+0xc65c` is not a complete click event: in the `373EA0` path, the store at `0x140374356` is reached even if the selected index equals the clicked index. `game+0x1039a` is only cleared on change, and `game+0x10be8` may already be `-1`. The same field is also reset **before** the row, without a click, at `0x14037403a` and `0x140361cbb`. Thus a `-1` transition has both false positives and false negatives. We need not invent a new button mechanism: the existing working button's interaction is the precedent, but its reuse and click ownership in the global sidebar need context-specific validation.

## Corrections

- **The row gates are not shown to be the same.** In `373EA0`, collision is followed by a direct byte comparison at `0x1403742f2`. In the alternate handler's actual row loop, collision at `0x1403620c1` is followed by the return of an indirect input call at `0x14036213b`, tested at `0x140362141`; the selection and counter stores are `0x140362193/19a`. The report instead cites `0x14036273f/7b1`, a later collision/input sequence that sets a local byte at `0x140362803`, not the row-index store. Its equivalence to reading or clearing `0x140a54e91` is **not established**. See [earlier review](almanac-export-review.md#3-click-paths-differ-between-the-two-handlers) for the reported input-slot symbol and limits.
- **Sound is conditional**, not guaranteed for every click: on the first handler's path, `0x1403742fd` can jump straight to the store and `0x140374315` can also bypass the later call. Do not use sound as a verified event signal.
- **Mouse position alone is not a click.** Sampling the cursor indicates a location; an actual press/edge, focus, correct native hit-test coordinates, skips and timing still need accounting for. The report's fixed `130+n×64` and 64×64 coordinates omit current scale and path-specific state; they are not a general live rectangle. A proposed observer may miss, duplicate, or invent a click. Nor is a blanket prohibition on consuming any shared latch on an Almanac-owned click demonstrated: native coordination requires correct target, order, ownership and scope. Consuming it indiscriminately on native clicks is unsafe.
- **'No clean observation point exists' is unproved.** The click-conditioned store paths at `0x140374347–35c` and `0x140362193–19a` are potential static leads for observing native row interaction, including unchanged selections, provided their guards actually pass. They are not proven safe or exclusive hook sites, and no patch or implementation is recommended. Any candidate requires full incoming-edge, displaced-instruction, flag/register, reentrancy, timing, input and cleanup analysis, and explicit authorization before modifications.
- **'Integration tests needed (authorized)' in the pasted result is not evidence that new hooks, deployments or writes were approved.** Prior permission covered a non-pausing read-only snapshot. A future context-specific button/input test must be explicitly authorized, designed and performed separately.

## Follow-up `7483770`: instruction-boundary corrections

The agent's [updated report](../export/almanac-evidence/native-click-close-agent-7483770/report.md) is preserved separately; its upstream commit overwrote the earlier report path. [Independent byte counts and in-export branch references](../export/almanac-evidence/native-click-boundary-independent/byte-counts.txt) correct its claimed hard blocker:

- The loader's `InstallInlineHook` in [`src/tesmioloader.cpp`](../src/tesmioloader.cpp) emits a **14-byte jump** and accepts a stolen span of **at least** 14 bytes, filling the remainder with NOPs. It does not require exactly 14 bytes, nor 14 instructions. The selected span must contain whole instructions and meet other safety requirements.
- `0x140374356` is a 6-byte index store and `0x14037435c` is a **10-byte** counter store, based on `RSI`, not `R14`. Together they form a **16-byte** instruction-aligned span ending at `0x140374366`. `0x140362193` is a 7-byte index store followed by the **11-byte** counter store at `0x14036219a`; together they form an **18-byte** aligned span ending at `0x1403621a5`. The report's 12/13-byte counts and claim that larger aligned spans are invalid are wrong. Other branch and instruction-size annotations in its tables are also unreliable.
- These are **only candidate spans**, not approved or proven-safe hooks. The first span's start is a target of `0x14037434d`; the alternate span's start is targeted at `0x14036214a` and `0x140362165`. Branches at `0x1403742ac/2f8` and `0x1403620c9/143` target the respective first instruction **after** those spans. These in-export references do not establish a complete incoming-edge inventory. The two displaced stores have no RIP-relative operands, but the loader's copy-without-relocation does not itself validate full control flow or make an arbitrary function-entry detour safe at a mid-function site. Preservation of registers, flags, stack alignment/ABI, original stores, call order, reentrancy, timing, input and same-frame behavior must be established separately. No changes or runtime tests are authorized by the arithmetic.
- The alternate handler's row checks the return of the input call at `0x14036213b`, not a demonstrated direct read of `0x140a54e91`; the text does not establish identical latch ownership. At `0x140362165`, `JZ` targets `0x140362193`, not `0x1403621a5`. Live-mode and sound paths can bypass sound while still reaching stores; they are not mandatory on every click. `game+0x10be8` has known pre-row writes and thus cannot identify clicks just by a read, even if every other writer were accounted for.

The report's overall **not yet safe** conclusion is still warranted, but its claimed impossibility based on exact 14-byte length is not supported.

## Follow-up `7a0ef4f`: candidate spans reassessed

The [agent's original reassessment](../export/almanac-evidence/native-click-hookpoints-agent-7a0ef4f/report.md) is preserved unchanged; a [later revision](../export/almanac-evidence/native-click-hookpoints-20261110/report.md) is also available. It correctly accepts the 16- and 18-byte instruction-aligned spans as meeting the loader's minimum and does not claim the hooks are proven safe. Our bounded [scan of direct branches](../export/almanac-evidence/native-click-hookpoints-independent/direct-branches.txt), limited to three existing text exports, finds branches to each span's start but none to their interiors. It does not cover the full binary, indirect targets, exception handlers or dynamically generated control flow. No runtime execution has been established.

Further corrections:

- In candidate A, **both stores use `RSI`**, including the `0x14037435c` counter store. The report instead describes `R14` as the counter base and asserts it equals the game object. Neither assertion is established by the shown instruction; use the actual bytes and live register provenance, not the report's summary. Candidate B's stores use `R14`.
- The recommended span starts at the **selection** store. If hypothetically intercepting at the later counter store instead, the first store has already run, so the report's instruction to execute *both* again and jump to the continuation describes a different detour contract. It has not established that either contract is safe.
- Normal x64 calling-convention tables alone are insufficient for an arbitrary **mid-function** observer: even normally volatile registers, vector registers and flags can be live across the overwritten point. Their actual liveness, stack state, shadow space and alignment must be analyzed for the actual stub and continuation, including exception/unwind and reentrancy. The current loader copies bytes without relocation and expects a conventional detour; it does not by itself provide a validated state-preserving mid-function callback.
- These paths cover **only native events that reach these guarded store sites**. Do not generalize to every visible icon, every input mode, alternate row or frame. In particular, the normal `361BB0` row is count-minus-one and both loops have skip and alternate-path gates; see [row review](almanac-export-review.md#1-both-row-loops-can-conditionally-skip-entries). A mouse position or latch value alone is not proof of a click. A mod-owned flag write can still have ordering, synchronization and lifecycle consequences; 'no effect on native behavior' is a goal, not a verified outcome.

The two spans are reasonable **static leads** for an explicitly authorized future proof of concept, not a completed implementation plan or a safe-hook finding. Native body replacement remains independent of click observation.

## Design impact

Keep all eight native icons and the Almanac button visible and clickable in one row. A mod-owned open state and context-aware coordination remain design candidates. Native body replacement, return, clipping and both handlers remain unresolved. Static code suggests possible click-path leads but cannot certify an added button, safe interception, or a complete content replacement.
